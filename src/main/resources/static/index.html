    How this demo works

    1. A simulated smart meter sends a reading every few seconds. The numbers below update by themselves.
    2. Open Simulator (top right) to trigger a power spike or load sample data.
    3. Unusual usage raises an alert: a pop-up, plus the bell next to the Simulator button.
